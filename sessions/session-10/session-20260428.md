## What I understood
- Backup and Recovery is important because it helps protect information when data is lost, damaged, deleted, or corrupted. It is basically the process of making safe copies of data and being able to restore them when something goes wrong.
- Automatic Backup and Recovery makes this process easier because backups can run on a schedule without needing someone to do them manually every time. This helps reduce mistakes, avoid data loss, and recover faster after a failure.
